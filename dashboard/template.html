Superstore · US retail · Jan 2015 – Dec 2018

Superstore Sales Dashboard

$2.30M in sales across 9,994 order lines. The business is growing, but discounting and returns are eating into the profit. Three findings below, then the charts behind them.

Finding 1 · Region

Central is losing money because of discounting in Texas and Illinois

Central has the weakest margin (7.9% vs 14.9% in the West). Texas and Illinois give an average 37–39% discount and together lost $38.3K. The other 11 Central states sell at full price, and every one of them is profitable.

Finding 2 · Pricing

Any discount above 20% loses money

Lines discounted more than 20% brought in $363K of sales but lost $135K. Tables, Bookcases and Machines earn +$46.5K at full price and lose $64.3K when discounted, so they lose money overall.

Finding 3 · Returns

The West sells the most, and it also has 4× the return rate

11.7% of West orders come back, against about 3% in every other region. That makes the West the source of 189 of the 296 returned orders (64%), even though it places only 32% of orders.
